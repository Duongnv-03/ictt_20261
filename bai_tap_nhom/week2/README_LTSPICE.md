# LTspice files for Week 2 practice

Open `Draft4.asc`, `PMOS_L.asc`, or `PMOS_W.asc` directly from this folder. Keep the three schematics together with `nmos_035.asy`, `pmos_035.asy`, and `5827_035.lib`. Their `.include "5827_035.lib"` references are relative to the schematic folder, so they work independently of the Windows username, Wine prefix, or checkout location.

Homework simulations and reports are organized separately under the repository's `SIM_HW/` folder, with one subfolder per SIM assignment.
